# Release Notes: v0.8.2.0

**Date:** September 29, 2026

## What's new and improved

- **Trial and license activation:** This release connects trial registration and license
  checks to Zenpo's live licensing service.
- **Smoother checkout and billing:** Selecting Buy, Update, Manage billing, Cancel, or Renew
  immediately opens a new tab. A brief "Opening your checkout" message appears before you
  reach the checkout or billing page. These actions also work in Safari and in private or
  InPrivate browsing windows.
- **More reliable subscription renewal:** Renewing an expired subscription now takes you to
  the appropriate next step for your account: an outstanding invoice, checkout for your
  current plan, or your billing page.
- **Clearer trial registration messages:** If your account is missing an email address,
  you'll see an explanation of why registration cannot continue. If the licensing service
  declines a registration, you'll see the specific reason instead of a generic error.
- **Less personal information shared during registration:** Trial registration no longer
  sends your IP address or customer name to the licensing service. License checks now include
  the installed package version.
- **Improved privacy:** Web parts no longer log site users' names and email addresses in the
  browser's developer console. We've also removed an unnecessary SharePoint request from
  each page load.
- **Updated help and license links:** You can open the
  [license agreement](https://intranet.zenpo.com/license-agreement) from the license link and
  access the [Intranet Suite documentation](https://help.zenpo.com/sharepoint-products/intranet-suite/)
  from each web part's settings pane.
- **Better expanded views for Org Chart and Full Calendar:** Expanded views now appear in
  front of SharePoint's navigation bars, keeping content visible. The Org Chart centers
  automatically when opened, and both web parts use slimmer scrollbars that match your theme
  on Windows.
